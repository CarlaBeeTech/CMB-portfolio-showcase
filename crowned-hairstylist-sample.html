Meet Your Stylist

A Passion for Natural Beauty

With over 8 years behind the chair, I've dedicated my craft to celebrating the natural texture, volume, and versatility of Black hair. Every client who sits in my chair deserves to leave feeling seen, cared for, and absolutely stunning.

I trained under some of the most respected natural hair artists in the country, and I continue to invest in education so I can bring the latest techniques and healthiest practices directly to my Kansas City community.

Imani Rose

Studio Policies

📅

Booking & Deposits

All appointments require a non-refundable deposit of 25% to secure your spot. Deposits apply toward your total service cost.

⏰

Cancellation Policy

We require 48-hour notice for cancellations. Late cancellations may forfeit the deposit. We understand life happens — please communicate early.

⏱️

Late Arrivals

Please arrive on time. Arrivals more than 15 minutes late may need to be rescheduled. Your time and our next client's time matter equally.
