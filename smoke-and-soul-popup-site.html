Our Story

From Grandma's Kitchen to the Streets

What started as Sunday family dinners turned into Kansas City's most talked-about pop-up. Every dish we serve carries the flavors of generations — recipes passed down, perfected, and served fresh at locations across the metro.

We believe great food brings people together. That's why we bring the kitchen to you — at farmers markets, festivals, breweries, and neighborhood spots you already love.
